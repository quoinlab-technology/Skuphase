# Tutorial: Generate a Lesson Note (Teacher Guide)

> **Audience:** Teachers and school admins using SkuPhase on a phone, tablet, or desktop.
> **Time needed:** ~2 minutes from scheme to downloadable PDF.
> **Screenshots:** Desktop captures at 1440×900, mobile at 390×844 (iPhone-class). Both variants shown where the layout differs.

---

## What you will produce

A complete, curriculum-grounded lesson note for one teaching week, reviewed and edited by you, then exported as a **print-ready PDF** or **Word (.docx)** document with your school's name and logo in the header.

---

## Step 1 — Open the Teaching workspace

**On desktop:** Sign in, then click **Teaching** in the left sidebar.
**On mobile:** Sign in, tap the menu icon (☰) top-left, then tap **Teaching**.

You land on the Teaching workspace ("Teach from the scheme, week by week"). At the top are three dropdowns that define your teaching scope.

> 🖼 `assessment_artifacts/screenshots/01_teaching_workspace.png` (desktop, full page)
> 🖼 `assessment_artifacts/screenshots/mobile_01_teaching_workspace.png` (mobile viewport)

**Key idea:** Everything below the dropdowns is filtered by your scope choice. Pick the wrong scope and you will draft the wrong lesson — check the three dropdowns before continuing.

## Step 2 — Choose your scope (Class, Subject, Term)

1. **Class** — e.g. `Primary 4`
2. **Subject** — e.g. `Mathematics`
3. **Term** — e.g. `First Term`
4. Tap **Refresh scheme** to load the official NERDC week-by-week scheme for that class/subject/term.

> ⚠️ If the screen briefly says **"No Internet Connection"** after you tap Refresh, your device lost the connection for a moment (or the server restarted). Pull to refresh or tap Refresh scheme again — your selections are kept in the page address.

The page reloads with the scheme loaded. The heading confirms your scope, e.g. *"Grounded in Mathematics - First Term."*

## Step 3 — Create the lesson plan

Scroll to **"Create a lesson plan"** and fill in the four fields:

| Field | What to enter | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Scheme week** | Pick the teaching week from the dropdown | `Week 1: Whole Numbers (Part 1)` |
| **Plan title** | A short title you will recognise later | `Whole Numbers (Part 1) — Week 1` |
| **Learning activities** | One activity per line (numbered helps) | `1. Count forwards and backwards 1–1,000 using the number line.` |
| **Assessment & check for understanding** | How you will check learning | `1. Oral check: 5 pupils read a 4-digit number aloud.` |
| **Instructional materials & local teaching resources** | One resource per line | `1. Number line wall chart 2. Bundle sticks 3. Pupil's Book p.12` |

> 💡 **Tip (local context):** The AI grounds its note in what you write here. Include local examples (chalkboard, bundle sticks, Nigerian currency, a nearby market) and the note will read like *your* classroom, not a generic template.

Tap **Create lesson plan**. The page reloads and your new plan appears under **"Your teaching plans"** as a card with a **Draft** badge, the week number, your title, and the scheme topics for that week.

> 🖼 `assessment_artifacts/screenshots/02_plan_form_filled.png` (desktop, filled form)
> 🖼 `assessment_artifacts/screenshots/03_plan_card_draft_with_ai.png` (desktop, new plan card with "Draft with AI" button)


## Step 4 — Draft the note with AI

On your new plan card, tap **Draft with AI**. The button shows a spinner while the server generates the note (this takes roughly 30 seconds on a good connection — keep the page open).

When it finishes, the page reloads and the card now contains a **Lesson note draft** editor with the full generated note: objectives, starter, explanation, guided practice, assessment, differentiation, and a teacher's remarks section — all grounded in the scheme week and the plan details you entered.

> 🖼 `assessment_artifacts/screenshots/04_ai_lesson_note_generated.png` (desktop, generated note in the editor)

> ⚠️ **If the note box still looks empty (or shows "[1]") after waiting a minute:** the generation likely failed or was interrupted. Tap **Regenerate draft** to try again. Your plan and its details are safe — only the note is regenerated.

> ⚠️ **If you close the tab or lose connection mid-generation:** the generation continues on the server. Reload the Teaching page after a minute — the note will usually be there.

## Step 5 — Preview and edit

- **Preview:** Tap **Preview lesson note** above the Save button to see the note rendered with headings and formatting. Tap it again to collapse.
- **Edit:** The editor is a plain text box — type directly to add your own notes. For example, append a local line like:

  `**Local note (Teacher):** Use bundle sticks from the school store. Counting chant in Nigerian Pidgin for the starter: "We go count am well well!"`

- **Quick refinements:** The card offers one-tap AI refinements — **Nigerian context**, **Make shorter**, **Simplify language**, **Add Activity**. ⚠️ These **replace** the current draft (your manual edits will be lost), so the app asks you to confirm first. Do your manual edits *last*, or after a refinement you are happy with.

## Step 6 — Save & submit for review

Tap **Save & submit for review**. The button shows "Saving…" and the page reloads. Your card's badge changes from **Draft** to **Submitted**, and your note (including your manual edits) is stored.

> 🖼 `assessment_artifacts/screenshots/06_submitted_card_exports.png` (desktop, submitted card with export links)

> 💡 You can still edit and re-save after submitting — the badge stays "Submitted". Your school's approval process decides what happens next.

## Step 7 — Print or download

Under the note editor you now have two export links:

| Button | Result |
|---|---|
| **Print / download PDF** | Opens/downloads a print-ready PDF with your school name, logo, class, term, week, and teacher name in the header. Ready for the staffroom printer. |
| **Download Word** | Downloads a `.docx` you can edit in Microsoft Word or WPS Office before printing. |

> 🖼 `assessment_artifacts/screenshots/mobile_03_export_links.png` (mobile, export links)

**Verified export contents** (real export from this walkthrough): school name ("Impath Academy"), school logo image, `Class: Primary 4 | Term: First Term | Week 1 | Teacher: …`, the full note including your local edit, and no SkuPhase branding in the body.

## Quick troubleshooting

| Symptom | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "No Internet Connection" page | Device offline or server restarted | Check connection, reload; your scope is in the address bar |
| Note box shows `[1]` or is empty after Draft with AI | Generation failed/interrupted | Tap **Regenerate draft** |
| Spinner stuck > 2 minutes | Request may have failed silently | Reload the Teaching page; check the card |
| Export opens a new tab instead of downloading | Normal on some phones | Use the browser menu → Downloads, or long-press the link |
| PDF/DOCX header shows SkuPhase instead of your school | School logo not configured | School Admin → School Settings → upload logo |
